## Influencing Accessibility Change – Mark Lynskey

- Accessibility is everyone's responsibility
- Circles of influence: you, your team, your organisation, more!
- Treat accessibility issues as **bugs**: POs hate bugs!
- Make Guidelines and Tools that **scale**

## Accessibility Success Stories – Michele de Bes

- Move Design Review to before Acceptance
- Expand Acceptance Criteria to include Accessibility items
- Ensure Strategic Alignment
- Continuous Improvement
- Design for everyone
- **Ask: can everyone use this?**
